# Project Roadmap

NepalKhata is developed in six distinct sprints, each focusing on a core area of the application.

## 🎯 Project Goal
To provide an ultra-premium, AI-enhanced hardware shop management solution that simplifies complex inventory and financial tracking for Nepalese hardware businesses.

---

## 📅 Sprint Schedule

### Sprint 1: Foundation (COMPLETED)
- [x] Multi-project solution setup.
- [x] Database schema & repository layer.
- [x] Authentication & Audit logging.
- [x] AI service foundation (Claude API).
- [x] UI Shell with MaterialDesign.

### Sprint 2: Inventory & Product Management
- [ ] Product CRUD with advanced validation.
- [ ] Category & Brand management.
- [ ] Barcode scanning integration.
- [ ] Stock adjustment & history tracking.

### Sprint 3: Sales & Invoicing
- [ ] POS-style invoicing interface.
- [ ] Fast product lookup (Manual + AI).
- [ ] GST/Tax calculation logic.
- [ ] Invoice printing (PDF/Thermal).
- [ ] Customer credit management.

### Sprint 4: Supplier & Purchase Management
- [ ] Purchase order creation.
- [ ] Supplier payment tracking.
- [ ] Reorder alerts (Low stock).
- [ ] Supplier performance metrics.

### Sprint 5: AI Insights & Reporting
- [ ] AI-powered sales forecasting.
- [ ] Smart search for inventory.
- [ ] Financial reports (Profit/Loss, GST reports).
- [ ] Export to Excel/PDF.

### Sprint 6: Final Polish & Deployment
- [ ] Backup/Restore functionality.
- [ ] Data encryption (DPAPI).
- [ ] UI/UX refinements & micro-animations.
- [ ] Final bug fixes & performance tuning.
- [ ] Application packaging (MSIX).
